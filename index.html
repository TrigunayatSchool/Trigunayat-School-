Admission Process

Simple steps to join us.

  1. Enquire
    Fill the enquiry form below or call us.
  2. Visit the school
    Meet our team and see the labs and classrooms.
  3. Submit documents
    Previous marksheet, Transfer Certificate (TC), Aadhaar card, birth certificate and passport-size photos.
  4. Confirm admission
    Pay the fees and your child's seat is confirmed.
